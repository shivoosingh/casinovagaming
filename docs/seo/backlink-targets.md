# Casinova Gaming: backlink and brand-mention targets

Researched 2026-11-25. Nothing below was contacted, submitted or signed up for. No accounts were created.

## Read this first

- **20 targets were confirmed by loading the page.** Another 12 are listed as "snippet" (seen only in a search result) or "unconfirmed" (something material is unknown), because those sites refused automated page loads. The list is not padded.
- **Review sites are a risk for this business model, not an easy win.** The closest comparable business found is BitBetWin, an aggregator that sends players to Juwa, Fire Kirin and Game Vault style apps. Trustpilot has removed its profile for breaching guidelines, and SweepsKings blacklists it at 1.2 out of 5 as a "middleman". Asking a mainstream sweepstakes review site for a review can produce a negative page that outranks the brand. Decide deliberately whether to invite that.
- **Regulation affects every pitch.** Search results show state attorney-general actions against sweepstakes casinos in 2025 and 2026 (New York, Tennessee, Minnesota, Kentucky). All outreach must be accurate about jurisdiction limits.
- **No free igaming directory that accepts operators was found.** Everything confirmed in that category is paid or for suppliers only.

Status: **Confirmed** = page loaded and says what is described. **Snippet** = from a search result only. **Unconfirmed** = stated gap.

## 7. Content worth linking to

Nothing above works without something worth citing. In order of likely return:

1. **"Who actually runs Juwa, Game Vault and Vegas Sweeps?"** A sourced map of the software, distributor and agent chain and who holds the player's balance.
2. **State availability and enforcement tracker.** Cite attorney-general releases and statutes, date-stamp it, and say where you do not serve. This needs legal review before publishing.
3. **Redemption-time and failed-load data.** Anonymised aggregates from your own transactions.
4. **"How to spot a fake fish-table agent" checklist.** Payment-app scams and cloned social pages.
5. **A plain responsible-play page** with limits, self-exclusion steps and helplines. Few sites link to it, but journalists and reviewers look for it.

## Avoid

- Paid "casino guest post" inventories and "DR 50+ gambling links" vendors.
- Private blog networks, expired-domain redirects, hacked or parasite pages.
- Incentivised or seeded reviews.
- Fake-address business listings and directory blasts.
- Describing the platform as "licensed", "legal in all states" or a "casino".
- Undisclosed Reddit, Quora or Discord accounts, and referral-link drops.
- Prize-sweepstakes directories such as SweepsAdvantage: a different meaning of "sweepstakes", so a listing would mislead.
- Paid directory tiers presented as editorial.
